# Varda - a portal you built pins itself, and only once.
#
# Stand somewhere with no pins of your own within a dozen metres before running this. The
# first line asserts an empty map nearby, and a base you have already marked by hand will
# fail it for a reason that is nothing to do with the mod.
#
# The DUNGEON half is not covered here and cannot be. Entering one needs a real Teleport pair
# inside a real location, and a scenario can neither place one nor press the key - so that
# half stays a thing to walk into by hand. This covers the portal half, which is the one that
# runs off the portal's own heartbeat and is therefore the one most likely to run twice.

clear
god
ghost
flatten

# Nothing of ours here yet, at the radius every line below uses. Saved pins only, so the
# player marker and a bed do not count.
pins 0 12

# `place` goes through Player.PlacePiece, which writes the creator id - and the creator is
# exactly what Varda checks, because pinning every portal the world streams past you would put
# other people's network on your map.
place portal_wood
wait 3

pins 1 12

# The interesting question is not whether the heartbeat pins but whether it stops. It runs
# twice a second for as long as the portal is loaded, so without the merge check this would
# be eight pins by now.
wait 4
pins 1 12

# A second portal well clear of the first gets its own pin rather than being merged into it.
# Fourteen metres forward is comfortably past the eight metre default merge radius, and the
# teleport is given a moment because TeleportTo only sets a target - UpdateTeleport carries it
# out over later frames.
move 14
wait 3
flatten
place portal_wood
wait 3

# One within arm's reach of where we are standing now, and both of them on the map.
pins 1 12
pins 2 40
